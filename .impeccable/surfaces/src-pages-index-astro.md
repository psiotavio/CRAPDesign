# Surface brief: CRAP Design (single page)

Scope and mode: Experience, with Read passages. One Astro page that carries the whole proposal and the working prototype of its loop. The layout being repaired leads; the interface recedes around it.

Audience, job, action: Milene and the class read the proposal end to end, then try one mission (checklist, peer review, reward, sticker pair glued in the album). Proof on hand: the proposal text only. Layout artwork is authored synthetic illustration of the planted problems. Missions 2 and 3 (login, product page) are drafted from the planted-problems table and labelled as a draft to calibrate in the pilot. Open decisions stay visible as open.

Unresolved: delivery format, implementation version, individual ranking, mission order, final name. The prototype uses fixed order P, A, R, C (the proposal's default) and says so.

Weighing (seed 2fb82bb3, assigned index 3 of my grounded list): the film strip, cracktro, orizuru, ekiben and terminal challengers lose on audience identification (design students know the print shop, not these). The patent sheet is competitive on clarity; its discipline of numerals hung on the parts they concern is raised into the build as numbered callouts tying each planted problem and each criterion to its place on the layout.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a printer's proof sheet, and the four CRAP principles are the four process inks. A broken layout is a misregistered print; each mission registers one more plate until the sheet is clean. It refuses the category default of a gamified landing page with badges and card grids.

OWN-WORLD: Neutral uncoated white stock, K black ink for text, and four flat ink fields at page scale: cyan, magenta, yellow, black (P, A, R, C). Black type sits on every ink field. Crop marks at section corners, a registration target as the mark, a color-bar strip as the progress bar, slug lines in small tracked mono on the margins. Archivo expanded for display, Source Serif 4 for reading, Spline Sans Mono only for slug and measurements. No cards of icon plus heading.

STORY: The visitor understands that a mission is a small, checkable correction, sees the layout get fixed plate by plate, then does it themself: tick criteria, send to a peer, get XP and a before/after pair glued into the album. They leave believing the album is the evidence of learning.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left, CRAP DESIGN in expanded black at poster scale with one sentence and the primary action (cumprir a missão 1.P). Right, the flyer as a proof sheet inside crop marks and registration targets, at about 70% of viewport height, misregistered with cyan and magenta ghosts. Beneath it the four plate buttons (antes, P, A, R, C) step the sheet through the missions, the ghosts collapsing as each plate registers, with the active criterion printed in the slug line.

FORM: Pre-press proof sheet and job ticket, position 3 of my ordered list (after the sticker album and the contact sheet); seed key 2fb82bb3.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
